GOOD TO KNOW

A few helpful answers.

How early should I order?

At least 3 days ahead for regular orders. For festivals, ideally one month ahead; ordering generally closes two weeks before delivery.

Are last-minute orders available?

Sometimes, subject to flower stocks and delivery driver availability. Please WhatsApp us.

Can I collect flowers in person?

Yes. Visit 709 Ang Mo Kio Ave 8, #01-2599, Singapore 560709. Normal hours are Monday–Saturday, 10am–10pm.

Can I order customized garlands?

Yes. Contact SG Flowers directly with your preferred flowers, colours and size for a quotation.
